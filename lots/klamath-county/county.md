# Klamath County

This folder is unincorporated Klamath County, Oregon. Sprague River is not a city. Planning, building, and the assessor are the county’s, in Klamath Falls.

The public tax-lot layer includes an owner name. It is still a map, not a deed. Assessed value on that layer was empty for the lot in this folder. The tax bill and the deed come from the offices below.

## Parcel map

County Land Explorer. The county says the lines are not a survey:

https://experience.arcgis.com/experience/dfbd28d66a73412ba34c47759c01aa94

Public tax-lot layer used for the lookup:

https://services.arcgis.com/H6Mh1bySxR4oHx6x/arcgis/rest/services/KC_Taxlots/FeatureServer/1

Klamath identifies a parcel by map tax lot, like `3511-03000-01400`, and by an account number. A listing that prints an “APN” may mean the account number. Tax maps are PDFs named from the township, range, and section, such as `35S11E30`.

https://taxmaps.klamathcounty.org/depts/assessor/gis_pdfs/Taxmaps/

## Assessor and tax

Property Search Online. An account page looks like `https://assessor.klamathcounty.org/PSO/detail/288003/R`.

https://assessor.klamathcounty.org/PSO/

Assessor, 305 Main Street, Klamath Falls, OR 97601. 541-883-5111.

https://www.klamathcounty.org/353/Assessor

Tax payments and bills: https://www.klamathcounty.org/263/Tax-Office

Pay or view a bill: https://www.paydici.com/klamath-county-or/search/new

## Zoning

The zone on the tax-lot layer is the district to look up. It is not a building permit.

Land Development Code: https://www.klamathcounty.org/725/Land-Development-Code

Forestry/Range is Article 55.2. Planning is 541-883-5121, option 4.

## Flood

The county Land Explorer includes a FEMA layer. FEMA’s own map is https://msc.fema.gov/portal/search. Use the tax lot, not a listing-site pin.
