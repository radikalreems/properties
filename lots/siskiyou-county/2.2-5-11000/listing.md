## Listing remarks (summary)

The listing offers both lots together in a quiet rural setting with year-round access. Remarks cite views toward Mt. Shasta, the surrounding hills, and the Klamath River area. Access is a private dirt road the HOA maintains. A well and septic would have to be installed. The remarks tell buyers to confirm buildability, utilities, and land-use rules with the county. Interstate 5 is the route toward Yreka, Mount Shasta, and Ashland.
