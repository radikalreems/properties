## Listing remarks

Nice property with countryside and hill views...adjoining lot 719 is also available...The Klamath River Country Estates (KRCE) properties feature gentle rolling hills and mountain views, the Klamath River Campground (Camp Host on site), a swimming pool and miles of equestrian trails. This rural location near the Oregon Border has unobstructed views of the sky and an abundance of wild animals. Association Dues are roughly $260 per year. Siskiyou County offers world class fly fishing, skiing and snowboarding. Other activities, just to name a few, include gold panning, bird watching (Bald Eagles, Great Blue Heron and Osprey), snowmobiling, camping, backpacking, river rafting and the World Famous Pacific Crest Trail.
