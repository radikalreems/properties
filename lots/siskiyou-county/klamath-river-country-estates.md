# Klamath River Country Estates

The cheap lots that mention a campground, a pool, and yearly dues are lots in a recorded subdivision. The campground is a shared facility for owners. It is not the thing being sold.

Checked September 25, 2026. Dues figures below are what each source printed. They do not match, and this file does not pick one of them as the bill.

## What is for sale

Each of these listings is a county parcel, usually about 1 to 5 acres, with its own APN. The ones we have checked are zoned R-R-B-2.5, rural residential. A house would still need a well and a septic system on that parcel. The campground's water and power hookups are not utilities for the lot.

The subdivision was mapped in pieces. One sold listing, a different lot than ours, gives the recorded description for Klamath River Country Estates Unit No. 4: Lot 770, map filed March 27, 1969, Siskiyou County Town Map Book 4, pages 148 through 168. That listing says the conveyance is a grant deed. Source: The Land Shop, APN 104-380-160, marked sold, HOA shown there as $158 per year. https://www.thelandshop.com/land-for-sale/250-acres-siskiyou-california-krce

County map books 103 and 104 are the assessor's books for this subdivision, west and east. Iron Gate Lake Estates is map book 102. That is a different subdivision, and those listings do not describe this campground.

A county appraisal of a different KRCE lot, 1.02 acres on Chinchilla Court, calls the estate fee simple and says the development has CC&Rs. It describes lots from 1 acre to 20 acres or more, and says the yearly fee pays for a clubhouse, pool, picnic area, private campground, and minimal road work. Value date March 2, 2021. The fee in that report is about $200 a year. https://bosagenda.co.siskiyou.ca.us/398964/398970/401221/401224/401289/2401289.pdf

## The association and the campground

The owners' group is Klamath River Country Estates Owners' Association, also called KRCE. IRS Form 990 filings describe it as a 501(c)(7) formed in 1967, responsible for the common area of a planned development of about 2,050 lots, and for recreational facilities for about 2,052 members. Address on those filings: 4710 Whitefish Pl, Hornbrook, CA 96044. Phone on the Cause IQ summary of the filings: (530) 475-3075. EIN 94-1709511. Fiscal year 2025 program-service revenue was $554,466, and total revenue was $559,578. https://www.causeiq.com/organizations/klamath-river-country-estates-owners-association,941709511/

That revenue is about what a couple of hundred dollars per lot would raise across 2,050 lots, before any separate campground charges. It is not a rate sheet.

The campground sits on that Whitefish Place common area, not on the vacant lots. Listing remarks describe it as members-only, with a camp host, RV and tent sites, river frontage, a pool, a clubhouse, and laundry. A directory page for the same campground says the rules allow owners to use it and do not allow leasing a site there. https://pinecliffresort.net/campgrounds-rv-parks/krce-campground-members-only

One of our own listing captures separates the two charges. The 731 Grouse Way remarks say the HOA fee is $250 per lot per year, and that camping hookups are $20 per night. So the yearly dues and a night at the campground are different bills, at least on that page.
