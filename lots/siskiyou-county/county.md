# Siskiyou County

Every lot in this folder is in unincorporated Siskiyou County, posted with a Hornbrook address and ZIP 96044. Hornbrook is not a city. Planning, building, wells, septic, and the assessor are the county’s, in Yreka.

The public parcel map does not include owner names. Assessed value, the deed, and the tax bill come from the offices below. GIS lot lines were digitized at 1:24,000, so use them to find the APN and then confirm acreage and boundaries with the assessor. Vacant lots often have no street address. Search the APN.

## Zoning and building rules

Zoning on the map viewer is the district to look up. It is not the general plan designation. Ask planning for the general plan and for overlays such as septic limits or erosion hazard. Those do not show in the zoning field.

Written confirmation is the Property Information Inquiry form, or planning@co.siskiyou.ca.us. The county says inquiry replies take about ten days.

https://www.siskiyoucounty.gov/planning/page/zoning

County code: https://library.municode.com/ca/siskiyou_county/codes/code_of_ordinances

- Title 10, Chapter 6, from Article 37, is the zoning districts.
- Article 53 is the B combining district. The number after B, such as B-2.5, is the minimum parcel size on a future split.
- Article 55, Table 10-6.5501, is lot width, height, coverage, and yards.
- Title 10, Chapter 3, is the road setback. It can be stricter than the zoning yard: the greater of 50 feet from the road centerline or 20 feet from the property line along the road, unless a variance applies.

## Tax bill

The parcel layer has a tax rate area (TRA), not the dollar amount. Get the value notice from ParcelQuest, then the bill from the Treasurer-Tax Collector. The county site lists “Pay Taxes” under services.

## Septic, well, and permits

Environmental Health reviews sewage and wells in this order: floodplain check, on-site sewage review, well permit, well install, water quantity and quality if required, then the sewage permit.

https://www.siskiyoucounty.gov/environmentalhealth/page/land-use

Perc-test and sewage forms: https://www.siskiyoucounty.gov/environmentalhealth/page/applications-and-permits

Nearby well logs: https://data.cnra.ca.gov/dataset/well-completion-reports

Building, Planning, and Environmental Health applications: https://www.siskiyoucounty.gov/community-development/page/online-permitting-portal

The county has said the first response on that portal can take about five business days. Community Development is at 806 South Main Street, Yreka, 530-841-2100. Hours posted on the zoning page are Monday–Thursday, 8:00–5:00, closed noon–1:00, Friday by appointment.

## Flood and fire

Flood zones are on the county map viewer and on FEMA’s map: https://msc.fema.gov/portal/search. Use the parcel, not a listing-site pin. Those pins are often off by miles around Hornbrook.

Fire hazard severity is a layer in the same county viewer. State Responsibility Area versus local responsibility changes which wildfire building and defensible-space rules apply. Confirm the current map before a building plan.
