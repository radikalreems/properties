## Listing remarks

These 2 parcels sold together equal 5 acres. Spectacular views of mountains and valley make this property a great option for building your dream home! Parts of this land are wooded, while there are gently sloping to nearly flat clearings for building. Who needs the gym when you can take a nice hike at home! This area is tucked away in beautiful mountains, near the Klamath River, and distance to HWY 5 is about 10ish miles. Location of lots is private, and easily accessible, at the end of a cul-de-sac. Klamath River Country Estates HOA provides yearly road maintenance, Klamath River access, a boat ramp, a pool, clubhouse, and camping hookups at a rate of 20$ per night. HOA fee is $250.00 per lot, per year. Klamath River Country Estates defers building guidelines to Siskiyou County codes, per HOA. Power pole is 200 to 300 yards away, per seller. Hookup availability is unknown.
